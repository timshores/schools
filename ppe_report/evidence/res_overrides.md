# Evidence record: the residential share and Proposition 2½ overrides

Written by `evidence/res_overrides.py` on 2026-11-09. An exploratory finding, which Tim asked for (#247, #248): correlations across the state's towns, not a cause shown. Spearman's ρ throughout; "held steady" means the towns' ranks on income per capita, property value per resident, population and density regressed out of both measures.

## The figures the map's note gives

- 351 towns, FY2025 residential share (the map's measure) and overrides passed from FY2005 on (DLS).
- Most residential quarter: 78 of 88 towns passed at least one override; least residential quarter: 28 of 88.
- Residential share and overrides passed: ρ = 0.53; held steady, 0.43.
- Residential share and non-residential new growth (applied to the levy limit, FY2006-FY2025, as a share of the prior year's limit, from DLS's New Growth Analysis): ρ = -0.81.
- Non-residential new growth and overrides, held steady: ρ = -0.38.
- Residential share and overrides, holding non-residential new growth steady too: 0.23, down from 0.43: new growth accounts for about half of the link. Override capacity: 0.51, then 0.23.
- The residential share and non-residential new growth move closely together, so how the link splits between them is imprecise.
