3. ppe_report/export_explorer_data.py

Why regional districts only:
   - Census revenue roughly equals spending for regionals (median ratio 1.06
     statewide FY2024), because member-town assessments are counted. For
     town-run districts the local share is badly short: 62 of 229 have
     revenue under 80% of spending (Amherst $18.4M vs $37.6M; Leverett
     $1.9M vs $3.4M; Springfield local revenue $11.8M against $809M of
     spending). Census's notes also say FY2019 on excludes tuition paid to
     charter schools from local revenue.

Checks:
   - Census C01 equals DESE Chapter 70 aid (c70aid) within 0.1% for every
     district FY2010-FY2024. FY2009 is 89.5% of DESE's figure for every
     district: the state paid $412M of FY2009 Chapter 70 from the federal
     State Fiscal Stabilization Fund (EOE fact sheet, 2009-05-21; DOR bulletin
     2009-13B), which Census counts as federal.
   - TOTALREV = TFEDREV + TSTREV + TLOCREV and TSTREV = its items exactly, so
     the nine sources account for every dollar. No negative values.
   - Census fall membership (V33) is within a few percent of DESE in-district
     FTE for regionals; DESE FTE used anyway so revenue and spending share a
     denominator. Hawlemont FY2024 ($52,078 per pupil) is real: revenue flat
     at ~$3.5M while enrollment fell.
   - Revenue per pupil >= spending per pupil in 367 of 368 regional
     district-years (median 1.32): it also pays for out-of-district tuition,
     buildings, state-paid pensions, which the PPE measure leaves out.
