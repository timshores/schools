2026-11-03 (Session 19) - Explorer: Special Education Spending and the Circuit Breaker

Tim (seahag prompt log #189, #190): more detailed special education costs for each district, with the
grants that go with them, after his PNGs from last fall's data storytelling collaboration
(special_ed_subcategories.png, special_ed_cb_timeseries.png, both citing DESE's RADAR page).

Changes:

1. update_dese_sped_data.py (new). Downloads two DESE sources:
   - RADAR's Special Education Direct Expenditure Trends workbook
     (https://www.doe.mass.edu/research/radar/direct-expenditure-trends.xlsx, kept in data/ as
     dese_sped_direct_expenditure_trends.xlsx), its "data" sheet: every district's direct special
     education spending, FY2008-FY2025, in eight categories (teaching, other instructional, in-district
     transportation, Massachusetts public schools and collaboratives, private and out-of-state schools,
     out-of-district transportation, non-public health services, spending from grants and revolving
     funds), with the categories' sum and the district's total spending. Written to
     data/dese_sped_expenditures.csv; the script checks that the categories add to DESE's sum column
     (they do, to the dollar). The workbook is dated in its header (2026-05-12).
   - E2C Hub ab34-d3ma, Special Education Circuit Breaker Reimbursements, FY2006-FY2026: students
     claimed, costs claimed, threshold, net claims, reimbursement by category, quarterly, extraordinary
     relief and supplemental payments. FY is the year paid. Written to data/dese_circuit_breaker.csv.
   data/dese_sped_sources.json records both.
   Neither source includes charter schools.

2. export_explorer_data.py: sped_pupils() (each district's FTE pupils in district and out, by year) and
   sped_fields(), called for every district and district average. Spending per category per FTE pupil
   in district and out, pooled over an average's districts (dollars over pupils); special education's
   share of total spending (DESE's sum over its total); the average district's pupils, for Totals.
   Circuit Breaker: what the state paid (quarterly + extraordinary relief + supplemental; the
   component reimbursements don't reconcile with it in 187 of 6,007 rows, because of prior-year
   adjustments), costs claimed and costs above the threshold, each per FTE pupil; students claimed
   per district. In an average every member counts, a district with no claim as paid nothing, from
   the dataset's first year (FY2006) on. meta.sped gives the years; two CSVs for the Data tab:
   districts-special-education.csv and districts-circuit-breaker.csv.

   Checks: Amherst FY2025, $8,152 per pupil x 1,213.8 pupils = $9,894,898 against DESE's $9,896,319;
   Circuit Breaker FY2025 $234,263 against DESE's $234,031 (rounding per pupil). Amherst-Pelham FY2025
   Circuit Breaker $801,627 against $801,866. 360 of 433 entities have both; the 73 without are charters.

3. mywebsite (explorer.ts, index.astro): two panels after Revenue on the Districts tab, "Special
   education spending per pupil by category" (stacked, legend toggles, comparison, trend, tables, the
   share of spending in the subtitle and tooltip) and "Special education costs the state paid back"
   (Circuit Breaker by the year paid, with students and costs claimed in the tooltip and full table).
   The eight colors' order passes the dataviz palette check's adjacent-pair tests on the page's manila
   (worst colorblind pair in the 6-8 band, legal with the stack's gaps, legend and tables).

Not done: federal IDEA allocations by district (not found as a bulk file yet).

   FY2024 = $234,031 (paid FY2025), FY2025 = $254,080 (paid FY2026). districts-circuit-breaker.csv keeps
