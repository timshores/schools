Summary:
Brought FY2025 DESE data into the pipeline's data layer without touching the
hand-built Excel workbook, drafted a standalone inflation addendum against the
published FY2009-FY2024 report, and built a five-slide PowerPoint for the
Leverett Education Foundation Education Forum (November 2026). The report

Findings that need Tim's decision:

   A. Cohort boundaries are fragile. calculate_cohort_boundaries() rounds the
      25th-percentile enrollment to the nearest 100. With the revised data
      (incl. the 15 late reporters) that percentile falls just under 150, so
      the Tiny bound rounds to 100 instead of 200, even for 2024: cohorts
      become 0-100 / 101-700 / 701-1400 / 1401-10K instead of the published
      0-200 / 201-800 / 801-1600 / 1601-10K. Leverett, Pelham and Shutesbury
      would move from Tiny to Small. Options: freeze the published boundaries,
      or keep the rule and explain the change.

   B. Window. With FY2025 the "15-year" columns become FY2010-FY2025 (keeps
      the labels honest) unless the baseline stays at 2009 (16 years).

   C. Prose. report_text.txt and the appendices state findings with numbers
      ("grown 3-6% from 2009-2024"); those need Tim's rewrite once the
      refreshed numbers exist, not find-and-replace.
