2026-11-14 (Session 26) - Explorer: Teachers and Staff, from DESE's Educator Reports

Tim asked for a DESE School and District Profiles teacher data section on the explorer's School districts tab
(explorer prompt log #269), after the rural schools story needed Leverett's teacher counts and salaries.

New
- ppe_report/update_dese_staffing.py: downloads seven statewide reports from profiles.doe.mass.edu/statereport/
  for every year each offers (one GET for the form, one POST a year): Teacher Data, Teacher Salaries, Teacher by
  Program Area, Staffing Retention Rates, Staffing Data by Race/Ethnicity and Gender (all staff), Educators by Age
  Groups (all staff), Educator Evaluation Data (all educators). Writes data/dese_staffing.csv (11,212 rows, 441
  districts, FY1997-FY2026; one row per district and year, 47 columns) and data/dese_staffing_sources.json.
  Staffing reports run FY2008-FY2026, salaries FY1997-FY2024, retention FY2009-FY2026, evaluation FY2013-FY2025.
- The salary report's 2012-13 prints one code against two districts twice (08530000 North Shore and Northeast
  Metropolitan; 09100000 Bristol County Agricultural and Essex Agricultural Technical): the four rows are dropped,
  with a WARN. More than six such rows stops the download.

export_explorer_data.py
- staff_fields(staff, codes): each district's and average's "staff": teachers and all staff (FTE, an average's per
  district), students per teacher (weighted by teachers), average teacher salary (salary totals over FTE), teachers
  by program area, staff by race and ethnicity and by age, evaluation ratings (weighted by educators evaluated), and
  the shares of teachers retained and licensed and of staff who are women.
- districts-staff.csv (the file's rows for the explorer's districts, FY2005 on); meta.staff and meta.sources.staff.
- STAFF_CSV and STAFF_SOURCES are inputs: the export stops without them.

Checked
- check_explorer_data.py shape: 0 problems. diff against the data before: only .staff on every district entity and
  average, meta.staff, meta.sources.staff, and the new CSV.
- The export clears src/data/massppe/evidence.json (the evidence scripts write it afterward); restored from git,
  since nothing it reads changed.

Leverett, for the record: average teacher salary $58,563 in 2023-24, eighth lowest of 396 districts (median district
$91,219; state $91,014); 15.7 teachers (FTE) and 8.6 students per teacher in 2025-26.
