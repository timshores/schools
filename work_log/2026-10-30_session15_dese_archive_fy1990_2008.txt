2026-10-30 (Session 15) - DESE per-pupil archive, FY1990-FY2008: normalized CSVs and source files

Tim: DESE's Public Records Center sent its older per-pupil expenditure files, back to FY1990, in
formats unlike the Hub data. Normalize them to plug into the explorer, publish the CSVs and the
source files on GitHub, and plan the explorer's integration (an on/off switch for the full data
set, and a year slider for the range).

Changes:

1. ppe_report/data/dese_archive/ (new): the 25 files as received (17 PDFs, 8 .xls workbooks,
   107 MB), with README.md listing each file, its fiscal year, what was used, the two measures,
   and how the scans were read and checked. The Windows Zone.Identifier sidecars were not copied.

2. ppe_report/update_dese_history.py (new) writes:
   - data/dese_expenditures_fy2005_2008.csv: functional categories and FTE, FY2005-FY2008,
     328-329 districts a year, same columns and labels as dese_expenditures.csv ("Classroom and
     Specialist Teachers" renamed "Teachers"; Total In-District computed). From the calcfunctional
     workbooks' long Data sheets: FY2005 (calcfunctional05final), FY2006 (data06 in
     calcfunctional08final, the January 2007 run), FY2007 (calcfunctional07dec), FY2008.
   - data/dese_day_program_fy1990_2006.csv: day-program per pupil by program and total, FY1990-
     FY2006, 327-358 districts a year; spending and average membership FY1997-FY2006. FY1990-
     FY1996 from the PDFs' Summary of All Day Programs tables; FY1997-FY2004 from the 5yrpp sheets
     (latest workbook per year); FY2005 total from "ppx vs funct"; FY2006 from ppx06.
   - data/dese_day_program_state_fy1989_2006.csv: DESE's statewide averages.
   - data/dese_archive/qa/: every PDF row with its checks and notes; spikes; functional and state
     checks.

3. Reading the scans: pdftotext -bbox word positions; figures placed by column centre; rotation
   corrected per page (header-line slope or column-by-column unwrapping, whichever leaves more rows
   with totals inside their program range); OCR'd codes repaired from names only on a strong match
   whose digits fit; split rows rejoined; empty rows dropped. Checks: name vs code (Hub, workbooks,
   351-town list); total within program range (vocational within 15%, as DESE's own workbooks
   show); FY1997-FY1999 PDF vs workbook (990 rows, 3,272 figures: 21 rows differ, 2 unreadable
   tokens, the rest DESE's revisions, 3 confirmed against page images); one-year spikes (8, none
   OCR). 11 FY1990-FY1996 rows read against page images: 2 corrected (Southwick-Tolland FY1990
   regular 3,748; Chelsea FY1996 special 11,057), 8 confirmed as printed, 1 split row rejoined.
   Workbook arithmetic checks all pass; FY2007 matches across the two workbooks.

4. Gotchas: the workbooks store codes below 100 as text ("091") and the rest as numbers (100.0);
   requirements.txt gains xlrd (for .xls); the PDFs' cover dates go in Last_Updated.

5. data/README.md: a "School districts before FY2009" section and a note on what the script
   changes.
