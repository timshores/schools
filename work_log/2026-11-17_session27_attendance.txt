2026-11-17 (Session 27) - Explorer: Attendance and Absences, from DESE's Student Attendance

Tim asked for absence data on all Massachusetts students (explorer prompt log #275, 2026-11-16), then for every
measure on one chart with switches to look at one at a time (#276).

New
- ppe_report/update_dese_attendance.py (#275): E2C Hub ak6h-9k7x, end-of-year rows for the state and every district,
  SY2018-SY2026, all students and 14 student groups, six measures. Writes data/dese_attendance.csv (rates as
  fractions of one) and data/dese_attendance_sources.json. School-level rows and the March snapshots are left out.

export_explorer_data.py
- attendance_fields(att, codes): each district's and average's "attendance" (rate, abs10, chronic, chronic20,
  unexcused in percent; days), and "attendance_next", the newest year a year past the axis. All students only.
  One district's figures are DESE's own. DESE publishes no counts with the rates, so an average weights each
  district's figure by its October 1 enrollment (dese_enrollment.csv total_cnt), over the districts with both.
- districts-attendance.csv (all students' rows for the explorer's districts); meta.attendance and
  meta.sources.attendance; SOURCE_URLS["attendance"]. ATTEND_CSV and ATTEND_SOURCES are inputs.

Student groups (#278)
- attendance_group_fields(att, codes, ys): the same measures for each of DESE's 14 student groups (ATTEND_GROUPS),
  over the attendance file's own years. Each entity lists its groups (attendance_groups); the figures
  (attendance_by_group) are left out of the entity's file (shipped) and written to districts-attendance/<chunk>.json,
  which the explorer loads when a group is picked. An average weights each district's figure for a group by the
  group's October 1 students (a _cnt column, or a _pct column times total_cnt). meta.attendance.groups names them.
- districts-attendance.csv has a row for each district, year and group (student_group), all students first,
  without the rows DESE leaves blank.
- Against DESE's state rows, pooled over every district: chronic absence within 1.1 points and days absent within
  0.4 for students with disabilities, English learners and low income students.

- attendance_max (#280): each measure's highest figure in any year, for all students or any group, on every entity,
  so the explorer's chart keeps one scale whichever group it shows. attendance_groups(src, codes) now returns all of
  an entity's attendance fields.

Checked
- check_explorer_data.py shape: 0 problems. diff against the data before: only .attendance and .attendance_next on
  every district entity and average, meta.attendance, meta.sources.attendance, and the new CSV. All 387 districts
  and 46 averages have attendance.
- The weighting, pooled over every district, against DESE's own state row: within 0.04 points on the attendance
  rate, 0.2 on chronic absence, 0.9 on absent 10 or more days, 0.7 on unexcused, and 0.3 days on days absent.
- The export clears src/data/massppe/evidence.json; restored from git, as in session 26.
