Tim (seahag prompt log #190, #192): the town files he downloaded from the DLS Data Analytics and
Resources Bureau (override capacity as a share of the levy ceiling, override and exclusion votes, capital
projects, debt and bond ratings, labor force), and Census figures on race, disability, sex and age.

Changes:

1. update_dls_fiscal_data.py (new): fetches ten DLS Gateway reports itself, the same reports as Tim's
   downloads (row counts match): certified free cash and stabilization balances with DLS's operating budget,
   long-term debt, authorized and unissued debt, Moody's and S&P ratings (one agency at a time, islCompany),
   net excludable debt by vote, the labor force (DLS leaves its codes blank; matched by name), the four
   Prop 2 1/2 vote reports in one file, and Schedule A Part 4 capital projects (one year and amount type
   at a time). Writes data/dls_*.csv and dls_fiscal_sources.json. DLS's "operating budget" equals its
   Revenue by Source total without enterprise and CPA funds, to the cent, so that report isn't needed.

2. update_acs_demographics.py (new): B01001, B03002, B18101 for the four five-year releases that don't
   overlap (2005-2009, 2010-2014, 2015-2019, 2020-2024), from the summary files (no API key): the
   Massachusetts zip's members by range request through 2020, each table's national file for 2024. Checks
   that sexes, age groups, and race groups each add to the population. Eleven towns the Census Bureau
   later re-coded have no 2005-2009 row; seven re-coded for 2020-2024 join by name through the population
   file, which lists both codes.

3. export_explorer_data.py: load_fiscal(), fiscal_series() for every town and town average: reserves at
   each fiscal year's end (June 30 stabilization balances and the free cash certified as of the next
   July 1), the operating budget, debt outstanding and unissued, capital projects by purpose, per resident
   and pooled; debt service and debt over EQV; the labor force (calendar year Y at FY Y+1, as the
   population); override capacity as a share of the ceiling and the ceiling per resident; vote counts since
   FY2005; each measure's newest dollars past the axis (FY2026), the newest year 90% of a group's towns
   report. town_ratings(), town_votes() (votes.json, every town's votes, loaded only when a list opens).
   load_demographics(), demo_series(): counts pooled, each release at FY+1. fiscal_shares() for four map
   measures. Eight DLS CSVs and towns-demographics.csv for the Data tab.

   Not built: a statewide budget-vs-actual gap. Schedule A's General Fund report offers Revenues and
   Expenditures only, no transfers out, while the operating budget includes appropriations to
   stabilization and capital funds (Amherst FY2024: $103.7M budget, $84.3M Schedule A spending), so the gap
   would count savings as money unspent.

   Checks: Amherst end of FY2026 reserves $12,223,807 free cash + $25,098,848 stabilization against a
   $105,444,302 budget (35%); override capacity FY2026 $36,299,102 of a $105,641,755 ceiling; S&P AA+
   (2026); 18 votes.

4. mywebsite: Reserves, Long-term debt, Capital projects, Residents by age, Residents by race and
   ethnicity panels; levy ceiling line, override capacity and the vote list in the levy panel; labor force
